- **Copernicus DEM (GLO-90):** © European Space Agency (ESA) and the European Union, licensed under the Copernicus Open Access Policy.
- **Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI):** Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [2024], processed by ESA.

### NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration)
- **NASA GRACE / GRACE-FO Tellus Mascons:** NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) / Center for Space Research (CSR). Open access public domain work of the U.S. Government.
- **NASA GPM IMERG:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center (GSFC) / JAXA. Open access public domain work of the U.S. Government.

- **Central Water Commission (CWC):** Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India. 25 Major River Basins of India.
