The SEVA First Innovation Challenge aims to encourage and
support young innovators in developing practical solutions to
problems of local and national importance. The initiative will
engage students from schools, ITIs, polytechnics, colleges and
universities, with a focus on feasibility, impact and
implementation. Promising innovations may be supported
through mentorship, technical guidance and incubation. The
Challenge seeks to create a national platform for scalable
solutions addressing community needs

=== PAGE 3 ===
Two Tracks to a Solution
Every problem statement under the Challenge may be answered along either of two tracks. This
recognises a practical reality of governance: not every national problem requires a new product.
Track A – Product & Technology: Developable and deployable devices, prototypes, software, 
materials or engineering solutions.
Track B – Business Model & Governance: Innovative service delivery, financing, institutional, 
process or governance models.
Equal Standing: Both tracks are evaluated on problem clarity, originality, feasibility, cost, impact 
and scalability.
Integrated Solutions: Participants may combine product/technology with business or 
governance models for greater adoption and scale.
