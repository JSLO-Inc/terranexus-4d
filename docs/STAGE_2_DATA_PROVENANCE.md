## 1. DUAL-SCHEMA FRAMEWORK: PROVENANCE VS. REPRESENTATION

To eliminate ambiguity between the physical origin of an observation and the mathematical technique used to visualize it, TERRANEXUS 4D enforces a strict dual-classification schema:

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    DUAL CLASSIFICATION ARCHITECTURE                                     |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| A. DATA PROVENANCE (ORIGIN & SOURCE NATURE)        | B. SPATIAL REPRESENTATION (RENDERING TECHNIQUE)    |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Direct In-Situ Measurements (PROV_MEASURED)     | 1. Observed Point (REP_OBSERVED)                   |
| 2. Published Geological Mapping (PROV_GEO_MAP)     | 2. Interpolated Field (REP_INTERPOLATED)           |
| 3. Remote-Sensing Observations (PROV_REMOTE)       | 3. Regional-Interpreted Template (REP_REGIONAL)    |
| 4. Derived & Administrative Data (PROV_DERIVED)    | 4. Numerical-Modelled Output (REP_NUMERICAL)       |
|                                                    | 5. Illustrative Conceptual Model (REP_ILLUSTRATIVE)|
|                                                    | 6. Unavailable / Unmapped Null (REP_UNAVAILABLE)   |
+----------------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+
```

### Institutional Provenance Rules:
1. **GSI 1:50,000 Quadrangle Geological Maps:** Must **never** be labeled `MODELLED`. They represent **Published Geological Mapping (`PROV_GEO_MAP`)** compiled from systematic field traverse mapping and petrological analysis. When rendered as generalized block formations where local boreholes are absent, they are rendered via **Regional-Interpreted Template (`REP_REGIONAL`)**.
2. **NASA GRACE / GRACE-FO TWSA:** Represents **Remote-Sensing Gravimetry (`PROV_REMOTE`)** derived from K-band microwave inter-satellite range rate changes. It must never be presented as local well depth (mbgl) or address-level groundwater depletion.
3. **Copernicus GLO-90 DEM:** Represents **Remote-Sensing SAR Interferometry (`PROV_REMOTE`)** of the Earth's surface canopy (DSM). Resampled to a $64 \times 64$ local metric mesh, it is classified as an **Interpolated Field (`REP_INTERPOLATED`)** with documented product-level accuracy.

## 2. STANDARDIZED DATA STATUS LABELS

Every scientific metric, overlay layer, and analytical card across all six modules displays one of eight immutable status labels:

| Status Label | Formal Definition | Operational UI Indicator | Allowed Use Cases |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **`OBSERVED`** | Direct physical measurement from in-situ sensor, borehole log, or ground survey benchmark. | Emerald Badge / Check Icon | CGWB DWLR water level, collar elevation, core drill run intervals. |
| **`PROCESSED`** | Calibrated satellite reflectance or telemetry corrected for atmospheric/radiometric effects. | Sky Blue Badge / Filter Icon | Sentinel-2 L2A surface reflectance, GPM 30-day accumulated rainfall. |
| **`INTERPOLATED`** | Continuous spatial field estimated mathematically between verified control points. | Teal Badge / Calculator Icon | Copernicus DEM 64x64 mesh, Kriged regional piezometric surface. |
| **`MODELLED`** | Deterministic solution of a physical PDE boundary-value problem under calibrated conditions. | Purple Badge / Network Icon | MODFLOW 3D groundwater head (currently marked UNAVAILABLE). |
| **`PREDICTED`** | Forward forecast generated by statistical, machine-learning, or trend-extrapolation models. | Violet Badge / Chart Icon | 12-month ARIMA rainfall projections, GRACE linear storage trends. |
| **`SIMULATED`** | Hydrodynamic simulation under synthetic boundary conditions or hypothetical stress tests. | Amber Badge / Sliders Icon | Thiem active pumping test cone (conditioned on active well selection). |
| **`UNAVAILABLE`** | Explicit disclosure that institutional records or satellite coverage do not exist for AOI. | Slate Gray Badge / Eye-Slash | Missing local borehole lithology, unmonitored rural revenue blocks. |
| **`STALE`** | Observation exists but timestamp exceeds maximum defensible currency threshold (>365 days). | Orange Badge / Clock Icon | Monitoring wells unmeasured for over 12 months. |

## 3. MASTER SCIENTIFIC DATASET REGISTER

### 3.1 Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) — National Hydrograph Network (NHNS)
- **Source Agency:** Central Ground Water Board (CGWB), Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.
- **Acquisition Protocol:** In-situ telemetry from Digital Water Level Recorders (DWLR) and manual piezometer measurements.
- **Reporting Temporal Frequencies:** Pre-Monsoon (April–May), Monsoon (August), Post-Monsoon (October–November), Winter (January).
- **Coordinate Reference:** WGS84 (EPSG:4326).
- **Measurement Units:** Meters below ground level (mbgl); Meters above Mean Sea Level (m AMSL).
- **Coverage & Density:** 33 core reference monitoring stations across all major hydrogeological terranes of India.
- **Known Limitations:** In-situ observations represent discrete point supports ($0\text{ m}$ radius). Spatial interpolation beyond $1\text{ km}$ is strictly gated by the Leave-One-Out cross-validation error threshold (MAE $\le 5.0\text{ m}$).

### 3.2 Geological Survey of India (GSI) — Bhukosh 1:50,000 / 1:250,000 Mapping
- **Source Agency:** Geological Survey of India (GSI), Ministry of Mines, Government of India.
- **Nature of Data:** Published quadrangle geological maps, chronostratigraphic columns, lithotectonic terrane boundaries, and metallogenic belt catalogs.
- **Spatial Resolution:** 1:50,000 scale (primary sheets) / 1:250,000 scale (national synthesis).
- **Coordinate Reference:** WGS84 / Everest 1956 datum.
- **Terranes Included:** Eastern Ghats Mobile Belt, Deccan Volcanic Province, Indo-Gangetic Foreland Basin, Aravalli-Delhi Fold Belt, Dharwar Craton, Singhbhum Thrust Zone.
- **Known Limitations:** Regional maps document surficial bedrock outcrop patterns and regional structural dips. They **do not establish local contact depths or joint spacing at an unmeasured borehole coordinate**.

### 3.3 Copernicus Global 90m Digital Elevation Model (GLO-90 DSM)
- **Source Agency:** European Space Agency (ESA) / Airbus Defence and Space.
- **Sensor Technology:** Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) Interferometry (TanDEM-X X-band twin satellites).
- **Native Angular Resolution:** 3.0 arcseconds in latitude and longitude.
- **Native Spatial Resolution:** $\approx 92.8\text{ m}$ North-South; $\approx 85.0\text{ m} - 87.2\text{ m}$ East-West in Central India ($20^\circ\text{N} - 24^\circ\text{N}$).
- **Vertical Reference:** Orthometric Height above the EGM2008 Geoid (meters).
- **Product-Level Accuracy:** Published absolute vertical accuracy: less than 4m at 90% linear error ($\text{LE90} < 4.0\text{m}$). Statistical specification across global dataset; not a per-pixel guarantee.
- **Known Limitations:** As a Digital Surface Model (DSM), elevations reflect the tops of forest canopies, industrial structures, and urban buildings rather than bare-earth terrain.

### 3.4 NASA / GFZ GRACE and GRACE Follow-On (GRACE-FO)
- **Source Agency:** National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) / German Research Centre for Geosciences (GFZ).
- **Dataset Product:** Tellus Level-3 Mascon Ocean/Land Grids (RL06.1, v04).
- **Physical Parameter:** Terrestrial Water Storage Anomaly (TWSA).
- **Spatial Resolution:** Native $3^\circ \times 3^\circ$ equal-area mascons sampled on a $0.5^\circ \times 0.5^\circ$ geographic grid.
- **Spatial Filter Support:** $\sim 300\text{ km}$ Gaussian spatial smoothing filter.
- **Measurement Units:** Equivalent Water Thickness (cm of liquid water).
- **Known Limitations:** GRACE observes regional mass redistributions across tens of thousands of square kilometers. It **must never be interpreted as local tubewell water depth or address-level water security**.

### 3.5 NASA Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) — IMERG
- **Source Agency:** NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / JAXA.
- **Dataset Product:** IMERG Final Run (GPM_3IMERGM v06).
- **Physical Parameter:** Multi-satellite calibrated precipitation rate and accumulation.
- **Spatial Resolution:** $0.1^\circ \times 0.1^\circ$ ($\approx 10\text{ km} \times 10\text{ km}$).
- **Temporal Resolution:** Monthly accumulated precipitation (mm/month) from 2014 to 2024.
- **Known Limitations:** Satellite passive microwave and infrared estimates can underestimate heavy orographic monsoon rainfall along the Western Ghats steep escarpments.

### 3.6 Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI & NASA ASTER Remote Sensing
- **Sensors:** Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI) on Sentinel-2A/2B; Advanced Spaceborne Thermal Emission and Reflection Radiometer (ASTER) on Terra.
- **Spectral Bands Used:** B2 (Blue, 490nm), B4 (Red, 665nm), B8A (Narrow NIR, 865nm), B11 (SWIR-1, 1610nm), B12 (SWIR-2, 2190nm).
- **Band Ratios Implemented:**
  - Iron Oxide Index: $\text{B4} / \text{B2}$
  - Hydroxyl / Clay Index: $\text{B11} / \text{B12}$
  - Ferrous Iron Index: $\text{B12} / \text{B8A}$
  - Gossan Alteration Index: $(\text{B4} / \text{B2}) \times (\text{B11} / \text{B12})$
- **Electromagnetic Absorption Physics & Penetration Limits:**
  - Electromagnetic radiation in the optical (VNIR: 400–1000 nm) and shortwave infrared (SWIR: 1000–2500 nm) regimes interacts with solid Earth materials according to the **Beer-Lambert absorption law**:
    $$I(z) = I_0 e^{-\alpha(\lambda) z}, \quad \delta(\lambda) = \frac{1}{\alpha(\lambda)}$$
    where $\alpha(\lambda)$ is the wavelength-dependent absorption coefficient and $\delta(\lambda)$ is the characteristic optical penetration (skin) depth.
  - In opaque, highly absorbing minerals (e.g., hematite, goethite, iron sulfides, and carbonaceous shales), $\alpha(\lambda) > 10^3\text{ cm}^{-1}$, attenuating incoming radiant energy within tens of micrometers ($< 50\,\mu\text{m}$).
  - In translucent, weakly absorbing minerals (e.g., pure quartz sand, unweathered calcite) within the SWIR atmospheric transmission windows, light undergoes multiple internal scattering and can penetrate several millimeters ($1 - 5\text{ mm}$) before escaping as diffuse reflectance.
  - **Fundamental Remote Sensing Boundary:** Crucially, regardless of minor variations in optical skin depth (micrometers to millimeters), **optical and SWIR satellite sensors detect radiation reflected or emitted strictly from the outermost physical surface layer of the Earth**. They cannot penetrate opaque regolith, overlying alluvium, agricultural soils, dense vegetation, or forest canopies to reveal buried subterranean ore bodies. Spectral anomalies indicate surficial mineral alteration and weathering halos; **they do NOT confirm the presence or economic grade of underground ore bodies**. Diamond core drilling and downhole geophysics remain mandatory for subterranean resource discovery and reserve estimation.

### 3.7 Central Water Commission (CWC) — India River Basins
- **Source Agency:** Central Water Commission (CWC), Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India.
- **Dataset Product:** Official Hydro-Geographical Basin Framework (20 official basins and composite drainage units).
- **Units Captured:** Basin drainage area ($\text{km}^2$), point of origin, outflow discharge terminal, major river reaches.
- **Composite Basin Precision:** Sub-basins like Bhimavaram are attributed to `East Flowing Rivers between Mahanadi and Pennar` rather than being erroneously lumped into the main Mahanadi river basin.

### 3.8 Subsurface 3D Cutaway & Fence Diagram Data Pedigree
- **Borehole Records Provenance:** Borehole records in `src/data/strataData.ts` (`AUTHENTIC_BOREHOLES` / `REPRESENTATIVE_BOREHOLES`) are **representative composite columns synthesized from published geological memoirs** (Geological Survey of India Memoirs, CGWB District Ground Water Brochures, and exploratory drilling reports). They carry the formal metadata status `status: 'DEMONSTRATION'`.
- **Coordinates & Collar Elevations:** Collar locations are registered to authentic geographic coordinates of prominent mineralized and hydrogeological districts (e.g. Khetri, Zawar, Kolar, Singhbhum, Pune, Ludhiana). Collar elevations are tied to regional Copernicus DEM terrain benchmarks ($\pm 5\text{ m}$).
- **Lithological Hatching Patterns:** Lithological patterns displayed on 3D fence diagram panels and 2D geotechnical columns are procedurally generated SVG/Canvas patterns conforming to international **USGS / GSI cartographic standards** (e.g. brick hatching for limestone/dolomite, speckled stipple for sandstone/alluvium, angled dashes for basalt/schist), rather than scanned raster photographs of physical drill core boxes.
- **Surface Land-Cover Texture:** The 3D cutaway top ground surface texture is an illustrative procedural HTML5 Canvas texture synthesized to reflect characteristic regional Indian soil and vegetation tones (Punjab alluvial fields, Deccan trap basaltic terraces, Aravalli quartzitic ridges), rather than a tiled, orthorectified Sentinel-2 satellite imagery mosaic.
- **Basal -55m Elevation Datum:** The flat bottom boundary at $-55.0\text{ m}$ elevation datum is a **technical model cut-off boundary** established to provide a clean, watertight Cartesian visualization base; it does not represent an empirical horizontal geological contact or crystalline basement unconformity. This disclosure is formally embedded in `Subsurface3D.tsx` metadata.
